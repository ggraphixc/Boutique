Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about shopping with ASIKO.

Shopping

Browse our collection, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You can pay securely with your debit card through OPay. You'll receive an email confirmation once your order is placed.
We accept all major debit and credit cards (Verve, Mastercard, Visa) through our secure payment partner OPay. All transactions are encrypted and secure.
You can cancel or modify your order within 2 hours of placing it. After that, we may have already started processing your order. Contact us at support@asikoboutique.com for any changes.

Sizing & Fit

Each product page includes a size guide with measurements in inches and centimeters. If you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up for a more comfortable fit. Our AI Stylist can also help you find the perfect fit based on your body type.
We offer free exchanges within 7 days of delivery. Contact us and we'll arrange a size exchange for you. The item must be unworn with tags still attached.

Delivery

Lagos: 1-3 business days
Other Nigerian cities: 3-7 business days
International: 7-14 business days

You'll receive a tracking number once your order is shipped.
Delivery fees vary by location and are calculated at checkout. Lagos deliveries start from ₦1,500. Free delivery on orders above ₦50,000 within Lagos.
Yes! Once your order is shipped, you'll receive an email with a tracking number. You can also check your order status in the My Orders section of your account.

Returns & Exchanges

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for unworn items with tags attached. Refunds are processed within 5 business days of receiving the return. Contact us to initiate a return.
Yes, we offer full refunds for returns that meet our policy. Refunds are processed to your original payment method within 5 business days.

AI Stylist

Our AI Stylist is a personal fashion assistant that helps you find the perfect outfit. It knows all our products, understands Nigerian fashion, and can suggest outfits based on your body type, occasion, and style preferences. Try it at /stylist.
