How to Measure

Use a soft tape measure for the most accurate results

Bust

Measure around the fullest part of your bust, keeping the tape level across your shoulder blades.

Waist

Measure at the narrowest point of your natural waistline, just above your belly button.

Hips

Measure around the widest part of your hips and buttocks, keeping the tape parallel to the floor.
