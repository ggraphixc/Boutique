Go to Products in the sidebar and click Add New Product. Fill in the name, price, stock quantity, category, and upload a photo. Click Save to add it to your shop.
Go to Settings in the sidebar. You can update your store name, description, logo, contact info, and all other details from there.
ASIKO uses OPay for payments. Customers pay online and funds go to your OPay account. Set up your OPay keys in Settings under the Payments section.
Go to Operations in the sidebar to see all orders. You can update the status of each order (processing, shipped, delivered) and customers will be notified by email.
The AI Stylist is a chatbot on your shop that helps customers find outfits. Go to AI Stylist in the sidebar to train it with your brand knowledge, FAQ answers, and style rules.
